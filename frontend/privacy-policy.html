Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 September 2026

SamarthAI ("SamarthAI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SamarthAI may collect, how it is used, how it may be shared with service providers, and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

Privacy Contact: info@samarthai.life

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a SamarthAI account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Account authentication information

Passwords are stored in protected hashed form and are not stored as plain text.

Profile Information

If you choose to use profile features, we may process:

  • Profile name
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo

Location Information

SamarthAI may process device location when you use location-based features such as GPS, family location features, nearby services, or service location features.

Location information may include latitude, longitude, location text, and the time the location was updated.

Family and Emergency Information

When you use Family Security or SOS features, information necessary for those features may be processed, including family membership information, emergency location information, emergency contacts supplied by you, and emergency alert information.

Service Information

If you use the Services feature, we may process information such as:

  • Service title
  • Service description
  • Category
  • Price
  • Service location
  • Service provider information
  • Service requests
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Complaints and reports
  • Portfolio/work photos

Images and Scanner Data

When you use the Scanner feature, an image selected or captured by you may be sent to our image-analysis service for the purpose of providing the requested analysis.

SamarthAI does not need to permanently store the scanner image merely to provide the analysis. Images should only be processed for the requested feature unless otherwise stated in the app.

Technical and Usage Information

Technical information may be processed as necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Providing SamarthAI features
  • Providing AI chat and image analysis
  • Providing family and location features
  • Providing SOS and emergency alerts
  • Providing local service features
  • Processing service requests
  • Displaying ratings, reviews, and service information
  • Sending service and account notifications
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse
  • Troubleshooting and improving reliability

3. Location Information

Location access is used only when required for features that depend on location.

Depending on the feature you use, location information may be used to display your position, update family location, find nearby services, or associate a service with a location.

You can control device location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location permission may prevent some location-dependent features from working.

4. Camera and Image Information

SamarthAI may request camera or image access when you choose features that require an image, such as Scanner or profile photo upload.

Camera/image access is initiated by the user and is used for the selected feature.

5. Third-Party Services

SamarthAI uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide certain functionality.

Supabase

SamarthAI uses Supabase for backend database and storage functionality. Information required by the application may be stored and processed through this infrastructure.

Google Gemini

The Scanner feature may send user-selected images to Google's Gemini service for image analysis.

Google Weather

Weather functionality may send location coordinates to Google's weather service to obtain weather information.

OpenStreetMap

Map functionality may use OpenStreetMap map data and related map resources to display maps.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Sharing of Information

SamarthAI does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with or processed by service providers when necessary to operate a requested feature, provide infrastructure, process an AI/image request, provide maps or weather information, maintain security, or comply with applicable law.

When you voluntarily publish information through a service listing, review, rating, complaint, or similar feature, that information may be visible to other users as necessary for that feature.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication passwords are protected using password hashing, and authenticated API requests use access tokens.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain account functionality, meet legitimate security requirements, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it should be deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Account deletion: Users may contact info@samarthai.life to request deletion of their SamarthAI account and associated personal data.

We will review deletion requests and, subject to applicable legal or security-related retention requirements, delete the account and associated data that we are required to delete.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Any such retention will be handled according to applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

SamarthAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to SamarthAI without appropriate authorization, please contact us at:

info@samarthai.life

11. Your Choices

  • You can control device permissions such as location and camera access through your device settings.
  • You can update available profile information through your account.
  • You can contact us regarding privacy or deletion requests.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
